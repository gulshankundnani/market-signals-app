About the signals

Readings, reference levels and volatility risk levels come from fixed technical and news rules applied to delayed, free data. "Indicator alignment" counts how many of those rules point the same way, from -100 (all bearish) to +100 (all bullish). It measures current conditions. It is not a forecast, a probability or a recommendation. Reference levels and risk levels are taken from price history, and prices can gap past them. The readings can be wrong.

Data and accuracy

  • Prices, news, dividends and fundamentals come from free third-party sources (such as Yahoo Finance, Google News, NSE files, Nasdaq and Investorgain). They can be delayed by up to 15 minutes or more, incomplete, wrong or missing, and we do not verify them.
  • IPO grey-market premium (GMP) is an unofficial market rumour, not an official or regulated price. Estimated listing prices and profit or loss are illustrations only and often differ from the real outcome.
  • Buying and selling pressure is estimated from volume. It is not exchange order-book data.
  • Backtests and the Track record show how fixed rules behaved on past data or past alerts. They are hypothetical, ignore real-world costs such as taxes, slippage and gaps, and do not predict future results. Small samples are unreliable.
  • Alerts can be late, missed, repeated or wrong, because refreshes run on a schedule that GitHub may delay. Do not rely on them for time-critical decisions.
